# 555558.com — Phase-wise build prompts

Concept: **555558 Prosperity Lab** — free tools and guides on how numbers sell (price endings, lucky digits, phone numbers, numeric domains, launch dates), monetised with Google AdSense, a lead-gen audit, sponsorships, supporter pledges, contests and hiring.
Stack: static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS, generated by `_src/build.py`, hosted free on GitHub Pages.

Global rules for every phase:
- AdSense head code on every page: `<script async src="https://pagead2.googlesyndication.com/pagead/js/adsbygoogle.js?client=ca-pub-6620975821265271" crossorigin="anonymous"></script>`, plus `ads.txt` = `google.com, pub-6620975821265271, DIRECT, f08c47fec0942fa0`.
- Top ribbon on every page: "Contact, if you are interested in this website/domain name/Sponsorship/Advertisement/Partnership" linking to https://web.works/contact.
- One inbox for every form. It must never appear in HTML: assemble it at runtime in JS from encoded character codes, post forms via FormSubmit AJAX, and use `data-mail` links that build the mailto on click.
- No trademark claim on "555558"; publish a trademark & copyright notice.
- Lucky-number content framed as culture, never prediction.

---

## Phase 1 — Research and positioning
> Research the number 555558 across commerce, business, AI, the economy and people: math properties (factorisation 2 × 3 × 92,593, 8 divisors, digit sum 33), the meaning of 5 (change) and 8 (prosperity, Chinese *ba/fa*), the avoidance of 4, charm pricing (left-digit effect, Thomas & Morwitz 2005; Anderson & Simester catalogue test), lucky phone-number auctions, numeric domain market terms (NNN, chips, AAAAB). Survey 25+ sites in numerology, pricing calculators, vanity numbers, domain marketplaces and number-facts sites. Output: a positioning statement, the feature list worth copying, and AdSense-safety rules.

## Phase 2 — Design system
> Create `assets/css/style.css` with tokens: jade ink #0E3B34, ledger paper #F6F7F2, gold leaf #C9A227, vermilion seal #B8322A, slate #3E4A47, mist #E3E9E4. Type: Bricolage Grotesque (display/UI) + Newsreader (reading). The signature element is a row of digit tiles where each 8 is a tilted vermilion seal and each 4 is a dashed grey tile. Responsive to 360px, visible focus states, reduced-motion respected, no horizontal scroll.

## Phase 3 — Layout and shared components
> Build `_src/layout.py` with `page()` producing head (SEO meta, canonical, Open Graph, JSON-LD WebSite + Organization + SearchAction, AdSense), the inquiry ribbon, sticky header with mobile menu, newsletter block, 5-column footer, cookie notice (Accept all / Essential only → non-personalised ads), and a floating "Support the lab" button.

## Phase 4 — Tools (client-side, `assets/js/tools.js`)
> Build seven tools, each with method explanation, FAQ (FAQPage schema), WebApplication schema, related tools, helpful poll and audit CTA: number analyzer (factors, divisors, bases, words, digit root, patterns, memorability, 8/4 prosperity score, prev/next, shareable `?n=`), lucky price finder (9/.99/.95, 8/88/888/68, shagun 1-endings, round, by market), margin & markup (any two solve the rest), phone number scorer (keypad converter), numeric domain grader, launch date finder, life path number. Add the live hero analyzer and a daily-number widget on the home page.

## Phase 5 — Content
> Write 9 original guides (700–1,000 words each, sources named in text, Article schema, byline, updated date): charm pricing 9 vs 8; the number 8 in business; numeric domains guide; vanity phone numbers; AI pricing and number psychology; 55555/555558 culture; launching on a lucky date; repdigits in branding; margin vs markup. Add `555558-meaning.html` (math, commerce, branding, AI/economy, people + FAQ), glossary and FAQ pages.

## Phase 6 — Lead generation
> Build a three-step "Free number audit" form (what to review → business details → contact) on the home page and a dedicated `audit.html` landing page with benefits, process and FAQ. Progress bar, per-step validation, consent checkbox, honeypot, redirect to `thank-you.html`.

## Phase 7 — Revenue and community
> `support.html`: tiers ($8, $18/mo, $88/mo, custom), use-of-funds (operations, promotion, hiring, prizes, research), pledge form, optional direct pay links via `DONATE_LINKS` in build.py. `advertise.html`: sponsorship tiers + inquiry form including website/domain acquisition. `community.html`: monthly contest with prizes and rules + entry form; careers list + application form. `videos.html`: click-to-load YouTube (youtube-nocookie) library configured in `VIDEOS`, plus a suggest-a-video form.

## Phase 8 — Trust and AdSense compliance
> About, editorial policy (sources, belief vs evidence, AI-use disclosure, corrections), privacy (AdSense cookies + opt-out links, FormSubmit, GDPR/PIPEDA/Law 25/CCPA/DPDP rights), terms (contest rules, supporter contributions), cookies, disclaimer & affiliate disclosure, trademark & copyright notice, contact page, 404, noindex thank-you. No ads between a tool's input and result; one primary CTA per section; no thin pages.

## Phase 9 — SEO and deployment
> Generate `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, `site.webmanifest`, favicon, logo and a 1200×630 OG image. Add `CNAME` (555558.com) and `.nojekyll`. Push to GitHub repo `webworksa1/555558-com` on `main`; enable Pages from `main` / root; point DNS (A records 185.199.108–111.153, `www` CNAME to webworksa1.github.io); enforce HTTPS. Then submit the sitemap in Google Search Console and apply to AdSense.

## Phase 10 — Growth (post-launch)
> Add per-number pages for repdigits and AAAAB patterns with unique computed facts, a numeric-domain sales table with dated sources, Chinese and Hindi translations, more pricing tools (discount, bundle, subscription), and monthly contest pages. Track conversions on the audit form and the top tools.
