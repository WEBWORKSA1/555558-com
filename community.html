Monthly contest: the best-priced idea

Each month we set a pricing or naming challenge. Example: "Price a Lunar New Year gift box for three markets and explain why." The clearest, best-reasoned entry wins.

  • First prize: a cash or gift-card prize announced with each challenge
  • Runners-up: a featured write-up on the site and in the newsletter
  • Free to enter; one entry per person per month
  • Judged on reasoning, clarity and originality

No purchase necessary. Open to adults where permitted by law. Full rules are published with each challenge and in our terms. Void where prohibited.
