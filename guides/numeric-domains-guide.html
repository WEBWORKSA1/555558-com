Numeric domain names: a buyer's and builder's guide

Why all-number .com domains trade actively, the vocabulary (NNN, chips, no-zero-no-four), what drives value, and how to build a real site on one.

By the 555558 Editorial Team. Updated October 27, 2026. 9 min read.

All-number domain names are one of the most liquid corners of the domain market. Short ones are scarce by arithmetic, they work in any language, and in Chinese-speaking markets numbers are often easier to type and remember than Latin letters. Here is how the segment works and what makes one numeric name stronger than another.

Why numbers work as addresses

  • Language-neutral. A number reads the same for a buyer in Shenzhen, São Paulo or Montréal.
  • Easy to type on a phone keypad and for audiences more fluent in Chinese characters than Latin letters.
  • Finite supply. There are exactly 1,000 three-digit .com names, 10,000 four-digit and 100,000 five-digit. Every one is registered.
  • Meaning by sound. Digits can spell phrases when read in Chinese, so some combinations carry a message on top of their pattern.

The vocabulary

TermMeaning
NNN, NNNNThree-digit or four-digit numeric names (N for number, L for letter)
Chips / Chinese premiumNumeric names with no 0 and no 4, preferred by many Chinese buyers
RepdigitAll one digit, such as 888.com or 55555.com
AAAABA run of one digit plus a different final digit, such as 555558
End userA business that buys a name to use it, as opposed to an investor

What drives value

  1. Length. Fewer digits, fewer competitors. Each extra digit multiplies supply by ten.
  2. Extension. .com dominates; .cn and .net follow at a distance.
  3. Digit mix. 8, 6 and 9 add appeal; 4 removes it; 0 is often avoided because it can be mistaken for the letter O.
  4. Pattern. Repdigits, palindromes, repeating pairs and runs (like 55555 plus a kicker) are easier to remember and say.
  5. Meaning. A year, an area code, a product number or a Chinese sound-alike phrase can each attract a specific buyer.

Building a real site on a numeric domain

A numeric name doesn't describe a product, so the site has to explain itself fast. The approaches that work: anchor the brand in what the number means (here, five 5s for change and an 8 for prosperity); offer a useful tool on the first screen so visitors understand by doing; and publish original, substantial content so the site isn't judged as a placeholder by search engines or advertising networks.
