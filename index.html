Questions

What is 555558.com?

A free lab of tools and guides about how numbers influence buying decisions: price endings, lucky and unlucky digits, memorable phone numbers, numeric domains and launch timing.

Are the tools really free?

Yes. Every calculator runs in your browser, needs no account and sends nothing to a server. The site is funded by advertising, sponsors and supporters.

Is any of this scientific?

The pricing tools are based on published consumer research and plain arithmetic. Lucky-number and numerology content describes cultural beliefs and is offered for interest and entertainment, not as prediction.

Who is the free audit for?

Businesses choosing prices, phone numbers, domains or launch dates, especially when selling across cultures. You get a short written review of the numbers you send us.

Keep the lab free

Supporters pay for new tools, research, monthly contests and the writers and developers who build them.
