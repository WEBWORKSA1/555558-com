Privacy policy

Last updated October 27, 2026.

What we collect

  • Forms: when you submit a form (audit, contact, newsletter, contest, application, pledge) we receive what you type. Forms are delivered to our inbox through FormSubmit, a form-relay service.
  • Tools: calculators run in your browser. Numbers, dates and names you enter are not sent to us.
  • Cookies and ads: see below.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Embedded videos

Videos load only when you click, from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com).

How we use your information

Only to respond to your request, send what you asked for (an audit, the newsletter), run contests and process applications. We don't sell personal information.

Retention

We keep form submissions as long as needed for the purpose, then delete them. Ask us to delete your data any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA, Quebec Law 25, CCPA and India's DPDP Act) you may access, correct, delete or object to processing of your data. Use the contact page.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and contests are for adults.
