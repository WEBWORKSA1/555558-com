How it's calculated

Life path: add every digit of your birth date and reduce to one digit, keeping master numbers 11, 22 and 33. Expression: convert each letter to a number using the Pythagorean table (A=1 … I=9, J=1 …) and reduce the same way.

Numerology is a cultural and recreational tradition. It is not a scientific method and should not guide financial, medical or legal decisions.

Questions

Why keep 11, 22 and 33?

Numerology treats them as master numbers with their own meanings.

Is my date stored?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.
