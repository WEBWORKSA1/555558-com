Lucky price finder

Enter the price you have in mind and who you sell to. Get price points that read well to that audience, with the reason for each.

Why these endings

  • .99 and just-below use the left-digit effect: buyers anchor on the first digit.
  • 8, 88, 888 and 68 carry prosperity meaning for Chinese-speaking buyers; 4 is avoided.
  • Endings in 1 follow the Indian shagun custom for auspicious amounts (101, 501, 1,001).
  • Round numbers signal quality in luxury and professional services.

Only suggestions within about 35% of your price are shown. Read the full charm pricing guide.

Questions

Will an 8-ending hurt sales with Western buyers?

It mostly goes unnoticed, but it gives up the left-digit benefit of a 9. For mixed audiences, test both.

Does this include tax?

No. Apply your ending to the price customers actually see, after tax where prices are shown tax-inclusive.
