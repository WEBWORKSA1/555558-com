Margin & markup calculator

Enter any two values. The calculator solves the others and links to price endings for the result.

Fill in any two fields, then calculate.

Formulas

Markup = (price − cost) ÷ cost. Margin = (price − cost) ÷ price. Price from margin = cost ÷ (1 − margin). Price from markup = cost × (1 + markup).

Example: cost 60 with a 40% target margin needs a price of 100, not 84. Read margin vs markup for the full table.

Questions

Can margin be 100%?

No. A 100% margin would mean zero cost. Markup has no upper limit.

Which should I report?

Accountants and investors usually talk margin; buyers and wholesalers often talk markup. Say which one you mean.
