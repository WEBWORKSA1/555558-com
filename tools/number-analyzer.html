Number analyzer

Enter any whole number up to 15 digits. Get its math profile, its digit patterns and how it reads in lucky-number cultures.

How the analyzer works

The math side is exact: trial division finds the prime factors, the divisor count comes from the exponents of those factors, and bases are standard conversions. The numerology root adds the digits repeatedly until one digit remains, keeping 11, 22 and 33 as master numbers.

The memorability score rewards fewer distinct digits, runs and recognisable patterns, and penalises length. The prosperity score adds points for 8 (and a little for 6 and 9) and subtracts for 4, based on Chinese sound-alike meanings. Both are comparative guides, not absolute measures.

Worked example: 555558

555558 factors as 2 × 3 × 92,593, so it has 8 divisors. Its digits add to 33, a master number in numerology. It is a run-plus-kicker pattern, scores high on memorability, and contains one 8 and no 4s.

Questions

What's the largest number I can enter?

Up to 15 digits. Larger numbers lose precision in browser arithmetic.

Yes. The page address updates with your number, so copy and send the link.
