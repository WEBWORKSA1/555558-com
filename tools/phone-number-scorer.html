Phone number scorer

Type a number, or letters like 1-800-GET-LUCK. We convert letters to keypad digits and score how easy the number is to remember.

Your score appears here.

What the score measures

Memorability is computed on the last seven digits, the part people actually remember. The overall score weights memorability at 60% and the prosperity (8/4) score at 40%. A high score is most valuable when customers find you through radio, outdoor or vehicle ads rather than a tap-to-call link.

Questions

Does this check if a number is available?

No. It only scores the number you type. Ask your carrier or a number provider about availability.

Why ignore the area code?

Customers usually know or assume the area code; the local part is what they must remember.
